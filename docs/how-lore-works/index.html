How lore works

Updated October 12, 2026

lore does anything for you and works with everyone you know. It lives in your group chats, your DMs and Slack. Ask for anything and it gets done.

It remembers the room

lore keeps what your group decided, who owns what, and what is still open. Each chat has its own memory, and one chat never sees another's.

It does the work

lore reads the web in a real browser, builds files on its own computer, books, drafts and follows up, then reports back in the same thread with what it did.

It asks before it acts

Sends, bookings and purchases stop for a yes from the person they belong to. Nothing goes out in your name without it.

It works for you, too

When lore can help one person, it reaches them 1:1. Your own lore remembers things just for you and does errands across the web, and what you tell it stays out of the group.

It keeps private things private

Each person connects their own accounts. Logins go into an encrypted page, never into the chat. Anything from your own accounts comes to you in a private message, not the group. Read the privacy policy.

Where it lives

iMessage and Slack. Text lore or add it to Slack to start.
