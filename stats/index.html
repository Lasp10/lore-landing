What these numbers cover

The member count uses admitted members mapped to a phone and deduplicated by phone. Unverified signups, waitlists and non-member texters are not counted as members. Friends and family of the founder are not separately removed.

Messages include onboarding and short replies, not only completed tasks. Processed messages include both inbound and outbound traffic. These totals are not completed-task or completed-introduction counts. Completed introductions are not measurable in this snapshot.
