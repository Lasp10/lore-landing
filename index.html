lore is an agent that lives in your group chat. It remembers what the room decided, does the work that comes up, such as research, drafts and checklists, and reports back in the same thread.

lore asks before it sends, posts, books or buys anything on someone's behalf. Each person stays in control of their own accounts. It works in iMessage and Slack.
