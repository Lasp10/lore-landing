lore

lore is an agent that lives in your group chat, at work or with friends. It remembers what the room decided and does the work that comes up.

It opens real web pages in a browser, reads them and takes screenshots. It works in its own sandboxed computer to build files. It prepares sends and bookings and waits for a yes before anything goes out. Each person stays in control of their own accounts.
