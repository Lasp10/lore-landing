01 · METHOD

Context first, then work.

Most assistants start every request cold. lore starts from the room: who is in it, what was decided, what is still open. A request like “send the update” already knows which update, and to whom.

Work is bounded and one mission runs at a time. A run counts as done only when it has evidence behind it, such as the pages it opened, the screenshots it took, or a document you can open.

A

Memory by thread

Decisions, owners and open questions are kept per conversation. One room's context never shows up in another.

B

Research and browsing

Opens public pages in a logged-out browser, reads them, and cites what it saw.

C

Documents and checklists

Turns the thread into drafts, briefs and task lists, and follows up on what stalls.

D

Approval gates

Anything that speaks for a person or spends money waits for that person to approve it.

02 · PRINCIPLES

Everyone owns their own accounts

lore knows who asked, what they shared, and where the result belongs. Your private information stays yours.

Claims stay literal

If lore did not finish, it says so. If it did, it shows the receipt.

The room stays the room

lore talks when it is useful and is quiet when it is not. It does not sit in the middle of every message.
