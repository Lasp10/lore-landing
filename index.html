lore is a private network on iMessage. Tell it who you need to meet, and it gets you there through people you already know.

The problem

Cold outreach doesn’t work.

You message fifty strangers and two write back. The people who could help you are buried under spam, and most of your connections are people you have never spoken to.

The right introduction usually comes from someone you know.

But you can’t see who your friends know, and nobody has time to ask around for you.

How lore works

Text lore who you need.

A cofounder who codes. Someone at Jane Street. An investor who backs AI. A student who got into Stanford last year.

Your lore asks theirs.

Every member has a lore. It asks the lores of the people you know, and they ask the people they know, until it finds the right person.

Nobody is named until both say yes.

No cold messages and no ignored requests. Every introduction is one both people wanted.

Helping is how you get helped.

Members who say yes to other people’s asks get their own answered first.

By invitation.

Every member was invited by another member, so everyone you meet is someone’s word.
