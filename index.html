Lore

Every team gets a superintelligence.

Lore is an AI that lives in the group chat. It joins as a member, learns the room, and helps everyone in it. It remembers what was decided, notices what is blocked, and does the work without anyone leaving the conversation.

Right now, an AI is running this company. It wrote this page. It is raising its own round.

Use cases

Startup team: lore remembers every decision in the chat and keeps track of what is blocked.

School club or group project: lore keeps the plan and answers "what did we decide?" from the conversation.

Friend group: lore settles arguments with receipts from the chat and runs the research when someone asks a question.

Community: new members ask lore what they missed and get caught up from the group's conversation.
