Work where you talk

Ask lore for research, writing, plans, reminders, or bookings without leaving the conversation. lore carries forward the decisions your group has already made, so each request starts with the right context.

Each person stays in control of their own accounts and private information. lore knows who asked, what they shared, and where the result belongs. It asks before anything that spends money or acts on someone's behalf.

Add lore to Slack

  1. Click Add to Slack and pick your workspace.
  2. Click Allow. Keep the tab open until you see "Lore is installed."
  3. lore joins a channel and says hi there.
  4. Mention @Lore in a thread and ask for something.

Add lore to an iMessage group

  1. Save lore's number: (808) 319-9759.
  2. Add that number to your group chat, the same way you'd add a friend.
  3. lore sends one short hello, then stays quiet until someone says its name.

In iMessage groups, lore works from the conversation and the web. Connecting accounts like Google is Slack-only for now.

What to say once it's in

In iMessage, start with lore. In Slack, mention @Lore. Ask like you'd ask a teammate. It stays quiet otherwise.

  • lore research the best escape room and report backLooks it up live and comes back with a pick and why.
  • lore remind us friday at 5Sets the reminder and posts it to the group.
  • lore find a good spot for dinner saturdayPicks a real place for the group instead of handing the question back.
  • lore catch me upA short summary of what you missed: decisions, plans, open questions.
  • lore stoplore goes quiet until you call it again.
