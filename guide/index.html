Welcome to Lore.

You're in. Lore is a private club that lives in your messages. Here is how it works, so you get the most from it from day one.

How it works

  1. Your drop

    Every day, Lore sends you up to three people worth knowing, chosen for what you're building and who you want to meet. Each one comes with a line on why.

  2. Pick

    Reply with a number. Your pick stays private: the other person gets a request from Lore, without your name.

  3. It's mutual

    When you both pick each other, Lore introduces you and finds a time that works for both of you, for coffee or a call.

  4. Your line

    One line, in your own words, on what you're building and what people should come to you for. It's how the right people find you. Tell Lore anytime to change it.

  5. Three vouches

    You can vouch for three people. They apply with your link, go through the same interview and hear back the next night. Choose well: your vouch carries your name.

Talk to Lore

  • Ask for someone. "I need a designer for a hardware startup" works. Lore looks inside the club.
  • Move a meeting. Text a new day or time and Lore takes care of it with the other person.
  • Take a break. Tell Lore to pause your drops, and to start again when you're ready.

The standard

  • Show up. A coffee you agreed to is a commitment. If plans change, tell Lore early.
  • What's said here stays here. Don't share other members' details outside the club.
  • Your details are yours. Your number and contact go to someone only after you both say yes.
