The standard

How lore is kept.

lore is a private network. These are the terms on which it is kept, and they apply to everyone in it without exception.

Discretion

We do not publish who our members are. Names, profiles and conversations are not shared outside the introductions a member agrees to.

The same discretion is extended to every member, and to anyone who is considered for membership.

Admission

Membership is by invitation or by application. Each person is considered individually, for what they are building and the spirit in which they would take part, not for title or pedigree.

A member’s recommendation is welcome. It is not admission. Not every application is accepted, and decisions are not explained.

Introductions

No introduction is made unless both people say yes. A declined introduction is never seen by the person who asked.

We do not arrange conversations with members ahead of membership, and we do not provide references. The network is experienced from within.

Conduct

Members keep confidences, answer the introductions they accept, and do not use the network to solicit, sell or recruit without consent.

Membership may be withdrawn from anyone who does not keep to the standard.

Leaving

A member may leave at any time by text. On request, lore deletes the member’s profile and what they have shared with it.

Those who keep it

lore is run by a small team. We prefer that the network speak for itself.

There is no deadline. Those who wish to proceed need only say so.
